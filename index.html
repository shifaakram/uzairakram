About Our Platform

The Institute of Productive Minds is currently a guidance and counseling platform. We provide parents and students with online facilities for timely education, computer courses, physical training, religious studies, social sciences, and community service opportunities.

Note: The institute does not operate as a formal school or college; instead, it equips parents with a clear roadmap to facilitate early-age high achievement.
